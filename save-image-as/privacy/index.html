Privacy Policy – Save Image As PNG, JPG or WebP

Last updated: 2026-10-14

This extension does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

  • An image is loaded only when you right-click it and choose a format from the menu.
  • The image is converted entirely on your device and saved through Chrome's download feature.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or remote servers are used.
  • The optional "access to all sites" permission is used solely to load the image you selected.
